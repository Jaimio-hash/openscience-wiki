# Temperature and electrical conductivity

Source: Al-Fartoos et al. (2024), A semi-transparent thermoelectric glazing nanogenerator with aluminium doped zinc oxide and copper iodide thin films. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s44172-024-00291-4

CSV transcribes columns A (temperature K), D (electrical conductivity S m^-1), E (SD) from the publisher Source Data workbook, sheets Supplementary Fig.6a (AZO) and Supplementary Fig.6b (CuI), rows 3–15. There are 13 temperatures per material (275–390 K), 26 rows in total. The article describes error bars as standard deviations from five independent measurements per temperature. The workbook provides the reported means and SDs, not the individual measurements. Preserve the reported SD; do not generate replicates or claim to recalculate it. Error bars are not confidence intervals. Lines between points are guides, not fitted models.
